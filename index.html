Your roadmap to becoming an Isko or Iska. 🏛️ University of the Philippines College Admission Test (UPCAT) The UPCAT is the college admission test for all UP System campuses. It tests Language Proficiency, Reading Comprehension, Mathematics, and Science. Your score combines with your High School GWA to produce your University Predicted Grade (UPG) — the lower, the better. ~100,000+ takers/year ~14% passing rate 4 subtests · ~5 hours Held annually (Aug–Oct) — Step-by-Step Application Process 01 Check Eligibility UPCAT 2027 is for current Grade 11 students (SY 2025–2026) who will graduate from senior high school in 2027. You can only take the UPCAT once in your lifetime — there are no retakes. 02 Fill Out Form 1 (Mar 3–30, 2026) Go to the official UPCAT Online Application System (OAS) at upcat.up.edu.ph and fill out Form 1 — the Application for First Year Admission Personal Data Sheet. Provide accurate personal information and your campus and course choices. The UPCAT application fee is free for Filipino applicants. 03 School Submits Form 2A (Until Apr 10, 2026) The online portal automatically forwards a request to your high school principal or school head to fill out school information and confirm your enrollment or graduation status. Your school then submits Form 2A (Senior High School Profile) and confirms you are currently enrolled in Grade 11 or have already graduated from senior high school. 04 Await Test Permit (July 2026) Details regarding the availability and release of your UPCAT 2027 test permit will be sent to you via email once available. Download and print it on white bond paper — do not resize, crop, or alter it. Bring it on exam day — no permit means no entry. 05 Take the UPCAT 2027 (Aug 1–2, 2026) Arrive at your test center at least 30 minutes early. Bring your printed test permit, at least 2 valid IDs, #2 or HB pencils, a black ballpen, and an eraser. No calculators, phones, smartwatches, or electronic devices allowed. Listen carefully to the proctor's instructions before starting. 06 Submit Form 2B & Update Form 1 (Aug 3–24, 2026) After the exam, update and resubmit Form 1 (Personal Data Sheet), then fill out and submit Form 2B (Secondary School Record). The portal notifies your high school to provide access to the Form 2A Portal and requests a certified true copy of your Permanent Secondary School Record (F137/SF10). 07 School Submits Final Form 2A & Records Your high school submits Form 2A and confirms you are currently enrolled in Grade 12 or have already graduated from senior high school. The Office of Admissions then receives a hard copy of your official school record, subject to validation. 08 Download PDF Copies of Forms Once available in the portal, download a PDF copy of your submitted Forms 1 and 2B for your records. Keep these safe — you may need them for enrollment verification and other official purposes. 09 Download Acknowledgment Receipt & Await Results Download the acknowledgment receipt once it becomes available in the portal. UPCAT 2027 results will be released via the official UP Admissions website. Log in using your OAS credentials to view your UPG and campus/program assignment. Qualifiers will receive enrollment instructions after results are announced. — Eligibility & Qualification Requirements 🎓 Who Can Apply for UPCAT? Current Grade 11 students (SY 2025–2026) who will graduate from senior high school in 2027 (SHS track: Academic, TVL, Sports, or Arts & Design). Recent SHS graduates who completed senior high school in the immediately preceding school year — subject to specific UP guidelines each cycle. Applicants must not have previously taken the UPCAT — you only get one chance. Applicants must meet the minimum GWA requirement set by UP (verify with the current application bulletin at upcat.up.edu.ph). ALS passers who meet the equivalent academic requirements may also be eligible — check the official announcement each cycle. 📄 Documents Required for Application Form 1 (Personal Data Sheet): Filled out online via the OAS during the application period (March 3–30, 2026). Form 2A (Senior High School Profile): Submitted by your high school principal or school head through the portal — not by you directly. Form 2B (Secondary School Record): Filled out and submitted by the applicant after the exam (August 3–24, 2026). Permanent Secondary School Record (F137/SF10): A certified true copy requested from your high school after the exam for submission to the Office of Admissions. Valid email address: All notifications — including test permit availability and results — are sent via email. Use one you check regularly. — Choosing Your Campus & Degree Program 🏫 Quota vs Non-Quota Programs — What's the Difference? Quota programs have a fixed number of available slots per campus. Admission is purely competitive — only the applicants with the lowest UPG within the quota are admitted. Even a passing UPG may not be enough if the quota is already filled by students with lower UPGs. Non-quota programs admit all applicants who pass — there is no slot limit. Any applicant who achieves a UPG at or below the program's passing threshold is admitted. Quota programs include: Medicine (pre-med), Engineering, Architecture, Fine Arts, Nursing, Physical Therapy, and several other competitive courses. Check the current bulletin for the official list. Non-quota programs include: Education, Social Sciences, Humanities, and other liberal arts-type programs. Applicants are ranked by UPG within quota programs — the lower your UPG, the more competitive you are. Strategize your program choices: put your most desired program first, but include at least one non-quota backup option. — UP System Campuses UP Diliman Quezon City · Most competitive UP Manila Manila · Health Sciences focus UP Los Baños Laguna · Agriculture & Sci focus UP Visayas Iloilo City & Tacloban UP Mindanao Davao City UP Cebu Cebu City UP Baguio Baguio City UP Open University Los Baños · Distance Learning — UPCAT Exam Details 📖 Language Proficiency Vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, analogies, Filipino language usage (tayutay, salawikain). Mobile phones / tablets / smartwatches — must be left outside the testing room or fully turned off in bag. Reviewers or notes — no reference materials allowed inside the testing room. Rulers, protractors, or compasses — not allowed. Electronic devices of any type. Extra answer sheets — these are provided by the proctor. — Test Permit & Admission Slip 🎟️ Test Permit — What It Contains & How to Download It Your UPCAT Test Permit is your official admission slip for the exam. It is generated after your application is approved and the application period closes. It contains: Your assigned test center — the school or venue where you will take the exam (may be different from your own school). Testing room number — the specific room in the test center. Exam schedule — the date and time of your assigned session (morning or afternoon). Your applicant number — your unique UPCAT ID. Photo and personal information — as submitted in your application. ⚠️ Important Reminders Print your test permit on white bond paper (A4 or letter size). Do NOT resize, crop, or alter it. Bring 2 copies if possible. Log in to your OAS account to download it when it becomes available — for UPCAT 2026, permits were released several weeks before the August 2–3, 2025 exam dates. — Typical UPCAT Timeline March 3–30, 2026: Application Period (Form 1) The UPCAT 2027 online application window opens March 3 and closes March 30, 2026. March 3 – April 10, 2026: School Confirmation (Form 2A) The online portal automatically requests the applicant's high school principal or school head to fill out school information and confirm enrollment status via Form 2A (Senior High School Profile). The school confirms the applicant is currently enrolled in Grade 11 or has already graduated from senior high school. July 2026: Test Permit Release Details regarding the availability and release of UPCAT 2027 test permits will be sent via email once available. Download and print your test permit on white bond paper — do not resize, crop, or alter it. Bring it on exam day — no permit means no entry. August 1–2, 2026: UPCAT 2027 Exam Days The UPCAT 2027 is administered on August 1–2, 2026 in testing centers nationwide. Two batches per day (morning and afternoon). Arrive by 6:30 AM for the morning session or 12:00 NN for the afternoon session. The exam covers Language Proficiency, Reading Comprehension, Science, and Mathematics. August 3–24, 2026: Submission of Grades (Form 2B) After the exam, applicants update and resubmit Form 1 (Personal Data Sheet) then fill out and submit Form 2B (Secondary School Record). The portal notifies the high school to submit Form 2A and a certified true copy of the Permanent Secondary School Record (F137/SF10). The Office of Admissions then receives the official hard copy for validation. — Common Mistakes to Avoid ⚠️ 12 Critical Mistakes UPCAT Applicants Should Avoid Submitting incorrect or blurry documents. Your application may be rejected. Use clear, properly formatted scans or photos. Missing the application deadline. There are no extensions. Set multiple calendar reminders for all key dates. Forgetting to pay the application fee. Your application is NOT considered submitted until payment is confirmed. Using a wrong or inactive email address. All notifications come via email — use one you check regularly. Not double-checking your personal information. Errors in your name, birthday, or school info may cause problems when picking up your test permit or on results day. Not printing the test permit properly. Resizing, cropping, or printing on colored paper may cause issues at the test center. Arriving late on exam day. Test centers are strict — latecomers are not admitted. Aim to arrive 30–45 minutes early. Forgetting to bring valid IDs. You need at least 2 valid IDs on exam day alongside your permit. Bringing prohibited items. Calculators, phones, and smartwatches are not allowed. Getting caught can disqualify you. Random guessing without thinking. The UPCAT uses right-minus-wrong scoring — each wrong answer deducts 0.25 points, while blank answers receive no deduction. Always try to eliminate options and make an educated guess, but avoid purely random answers. Not choosing campus/course options strategically. Research quota vs non-quota programs and rank your choices wisely. Put realistic options as backups. Studying only a few weeks before the exam. Start your review at least 3–6 months before exam day for the best results. — Understanding UPG 🔢 How Is the UPG Computed? The University Predicted Grade (UPG) is the score used by UP to rank all UPCAT applicants. It combines two components: UPCAT Score (approx. 60% weight): Based on your Transmuted Score (TS), which is derived from your raw scores across all 4 subtests (Language Proficiency, Reading Comprehension, Math, Science). High School GWA (approx. 40% weight): Your cumulative Grade 11 and (partial) Grade 12 GWA, converted to the UP 1.0–5.0 grade scale. Lower UPG = Better performance. A UPG of 1.0 is the best; 5.0 is the lowest acceptable. Important: The exact UPG formula is not officially published by UP. The weights above are community estimates based on available data from UP alumni. The formula may differ slightly from year to year. 💡 Use the UPG Calculator Use the UPG Calculator tab in this vault to get an estimated UPG based on your UPCAT percentile rank and GWA. Log in to your OAS account using the same credentials from your application to view your personalized results and UPG. Results show whether you passed, are on the waitlist, or did not pass for each of your chosen programs. Passers receive their UPG and instructions for the enrollment process. Applicants who did not pass may file an appeal for reconsideration within the specified period — check the campus website for the process. Even if you don't pass, explore other pathways into UP — UPCAT-free enrollment programs exist for some units (e.g., UP Open University online courses, transfer student programs). — FAQs Is the UPCAT free to take? No — there is an application fee, but qualified low-income applicants may apply for a fee waiver using a Notice of Assessment (NOA) from DSWD or proof of CHED scholarship. The fee is typically below ₱500. Check the official UP Admissions site for the current fee. What are the UPCAT exam materials I must bring? You must bring: (1) printed UPCAT Test Permit, (2) at least two valid IDs, (3) #2 or HB pencils (at least 3), (4) a black ballpen, and (5) an eraser. Calculators, phones, smartwatches, and all electronic devices are strictly prohibited inside the testing room. Is there a right-minus-wrong penalty in the UPCAT? Yes. The UPCAT uses a right-minus-wrong scoring system. Each incorrect answer deducts 0.25 points from your score, while unanswered items receive no deduction. Because of this, examinees are encouraged to make educated guesses — eliminate obviously wrong choices first to improve your odds — rather than leaving items blank or guessing randomly.
